수업개요 및 목표
수업개요 | This technical class covers all hardware and software aspects of Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR). The class is concerned with not only the scientific and technological background of the aspects, but also with practical implementation skills in a hands-on manner.
수업목표 | 1. Gathering basic understandings of technical and scientific principles for building the hardware and software components required for VR/AR system. 2. Gaining practical experiences of designing, implementing, and evaluating a VR/AR system
