수업개요 및 목표
수업개요 | In this course, students learn how to secure modern computer programs and systems; the course will cover the basic concepts of cyber security as well as the application of cyber security schemes and techniques in specific domains such as the Internet of Things (IoT) security, mobile systems, web applications, machine learning and AI security, and cyber forensics. -IMPORTANT NOTE: The syllabus below is tentative and subject to change.
수업목표 | At the end of this course, students know: 1) basic cyber security terms and concepts (e.g., CIA triad, OWASP, malware, etc.) 2) emerging security issues in modern cyber systems (e.g., IoT, web, AI, etc.)
