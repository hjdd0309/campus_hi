수업개요 및 목표
수업개요 | This course introduces and provides practical programming concepts for game components, data and signal analysis, and artificial intelligence using game programming. It explores fundamental theories of signal and image processing, analysis, machine learning, and game vision, focusing on their applications within diverse fields of artificial intelligence.
수업목표 | This course aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of artificial intelligence and data analysis through both theoretical knowledge and practical programming in game development.
