수업개요 및 목표
수업개요 | This course introduces the core architectures, training methods, tuning strategies, and evaluation techniques of modern AI models, with a focus on Attention, Transformers, and Large Language Models (LLMs). It also covers recent extensions of LLMs, including reasoning, Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), Agentic AI, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Students will gain basic hands-on experience through assignments using PyTorch and Hugging Face. --- Lectures will be delivered in Korean, while lecture slides will be provided in English. Exam questions will be provided only in English. Students may write exam answers in either Korean or English. 강의는 한국어로 진행되며, 강의 슬라이드는 영어로 제공됩니다. 시험문제는 영어로만 제공됩니다. 시험 답안은 한국어 또는 영어로 작성할 수 있습니다.
수업목표 | By the end of this course, students will understand the core principles of Attention, Transformers, and LLMs, and gain a conceptual understanding of how modern AI models are developed, adapted, and evaluated. They will also learn key concepts in reasoning, multimodal AI, Agentic AI, and RAG, while building basic practical familiarity through PyTorch and Hugging Face-based assignments.
