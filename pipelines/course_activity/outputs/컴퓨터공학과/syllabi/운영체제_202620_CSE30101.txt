수업개요 및 목표
수업개요 | This course explores the fundamental principles of modern operating systems, with a focus on Unix-like systems and their role in modern computing platforms. --- Lectures will be delivered in Korean, while lecture slides will be provided in English. Exam questions will be provided only in English. Students may write exam answers in either Korean or English. 강의는 한국어로 진행되며, 강의 슬라이드는 영어로 제공됩니다. 시험문제는 영어로만 제공됩니다. 시험 답안은 한국어 또는 영어로 작성할 수 있습니다.
수업목표 | Students will learn the core principles of operating system design and implementation, including kernel structure, process management, memory management, and file and storage management. Students will also understand how operating systems manage hardware resources and provide execution environments for programs.
